WELCOME TO QUINCE

Two neighbourhood places. One love for good food.

Quince is home to Quince Bakery and Clementine, two neighbourhood places created by pastry chef, baker and author Anna Higham. Both are rooted in British produce and food traditions, the changing seasons, and close relationships with the people who grow, mill and make our ingredients.

OUR STORY

Good food begins with good ingredients.

Quince was founded by pastry chef and baker Anna Higham to celebrate British produce and baking traditions. That approach now runs through both Quince Bakery and Clementine, our two places on New North Road in Islington.

Everything we create is shaped by the ingredients available to us and by the people who grow, mill and produce them.
